Delete your coook account

This page explains how to delete your coook account and the data linked to it. It applies to the coook AI app on Google Play and the App Store, the Mac app and the web version, which all share one account.

Delete it in the app

  • Open coook AI and tap the person icon at the top right.
  • Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

Delete it without the app

Email support@coook.ai from the address you sign in with and write "Delete my account". We confirm by replying to that address, then delete the account within 15 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and sign-in tokens.
  • Your conversations, tasks, files and saved memory in coook.
  • If you have a paid plan, its renewal is cancelled first.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Account details are removed within 30 days after the account is deleted.
  • Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Usage details used for allowance and safety limits expire on their normal schedule (up to 45 days for hosted model usage).

Full details are in the Privacy Policy, section 5.
